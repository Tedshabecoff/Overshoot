About Overshoot

Overshoot is a strategy game about running a fossil-dependent economy and trying to grow it without overheating the planet. You build power, hold a coalition together, and race your own lock-in.

It exists to make one idea you can feel: cheap now is expensive later. The plant you build cheaply today is the one you can't afford to shut down tomorrow.

Built by Ted Shabecoff.

INHERITED COAL ECONOMY — JANUARY 2025

You've just taken office. Five fossil plants power the economy. They're cheap, they're popular, and they belong to the people who built their lives around them.

You have three tools for the transition:

🏭 FOSSIL
Cheap (11). Grows the economy. Jobs boost support. But every decade it runs, retirement gets harder and costlier.
⚡ CLEAN
Better long-term, but starts at 28. Fund R&D to cut the price. Dispatches first — cuts emissions before you retire anything.
🌲 FOREST
Cheap mitigation (8). Absorbs 4 emissions per decade. But forests take land you can't use for anything else.

Mandate: triple GDP, stay under +2.0° warming, keep political support. You have 15 decades. The planet has tipping points — you'll find out where they are by crossing them.

Cheap now is expensive later.
